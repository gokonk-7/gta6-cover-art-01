Vice Cover Studio turns your photo and a caption into GTA-style poster cover art — a multi-panel Vice City cover with bold condensed lettering and a sunset or neon grade — and exports it as a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. It runs entirely offline in a desktop browser from static files: your photos never leave your machine. Pick one of four layouts (Triptych, Comic, Hero+Duo, Quad), drop a photo into each panel (or use a bundled sample), drag to pan and zoom to crop, edit the title and subtitle, pick a grade (Sunset, Neon, Golden Hour, Noir), and export with one click. The backdrop — synthwave sun, skyline and palm silhouettes over a striped grid horizon — is drawn procedurally on canvas, so every cover is original art with no Rockstar logos, artwork or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080, load a photo, type a caption, and hit an export button. No build step, no network, no dependencies.

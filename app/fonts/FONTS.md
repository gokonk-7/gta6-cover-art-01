# Fonts

The cover lettering uses **Anton** by Vernon Adams, licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1, sourced from the Google Fonts project at
github.com/google/fonts. Anton is a condensed, extra-bold sans-serif — the
right shape for poster-style titles.

`Anton-Regular.ttf` in this folder is a subset of the full Anton font,
made in this repository's build session with fontTools: Latin letters,
digits and punctuation only (U+0020–007E and U+00A0–00FF, with kerning and
ligature features kept). It is the same typeface, trimmed to the characters
the app needs, so the app stays small and fully offline. The full font and
its license are available from the Google Fonts repository.
